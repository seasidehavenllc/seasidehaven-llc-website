FINAL DECREE OF DIVORCE FROM BONDS OF MATRIMONY

THIS CAUSE, which has been properly docketed and matured, came to be heard upon the plaintiff’s Complaint; upon process duly issued and accepted by the defendant under Section 20-99.1:1 of the Code of Virginia (1950); upon defendant’s “Affidavit of Acceptance of Service and Waiver” wherein the defendant accepted service of the Summons and Complaint and waived maturity of this cause and right to file an answer or other responsive pleading and appeared generally; upon affidavit duly taken before a Notary Public after waiver of notice and of the maturity of the cause by defendant and leave of Court hereby given pursuant to Rule of Court 4:5(a), and filed herein.

UPON considering the evidence independent of the admissions of the parties, the Court adjudges these facts: that the parties were lawfully married on April 27, 2019, in Carpinteria, California; that both parties are over the age of eighteen; that Michael Jesse George Burrola is a member of the Armed Forces of the United States; that the plaintiff was domiciled in and an actual bona fide resident of Virginia for at least six months immediately preceding the commencement of this suit; that the plaintiff was a resident of Bedford County, Virginia, at the time this suit for divorce was commenced; that the defendant was a resident of Bedford County, Virginia, at the commencement of this suit; that the parties last cohabited as husband and wife in Bedford County, Virginia; that the parties had one child born of the marriage, namely Landyn Kai Burrola, born February 14, 2022; that no additional children are expected; that the parties separated on or about April 1, 2025, with the intent to permanently remain separate and apart, and have lived separate and apart without cohabitation and without interruption since that separation, a period of one year and more; that the plaintiff has intended for at least the last year that the separation be a permanent one; and that no reconciliation is probable.

UPON these findings, the Court DECREES that the bond of matrimony created by the marriage between these parties is dissolved pursuant to Virginia Code § 20-91(A)(9), and they are divorced from each other.

Beneficiary designations for any death benefit, as defined in subsection B of § 20-111.1 of the Code of Virginia, made payable to a former spouse may or may not be automatically revoked by operation of law upon the entry of a final decree of annulment or divorce. If a party intends to revoke any beneficiary designation made payable to a former spouse following the annulment or divorce, the party is responsible for following any and all instructions to change such beneficiary designation given by the provider of the death benefit. Otherwise, existing beneficiary designations may remain in full force and effect after the entry of a final decree of annulment or divorce.

The Clerk shall strike the matter from the docket and furnish a certified copy of this decree to plaintiff at 1069 Estates Road, Moneta, VA 24121, and one to the defendant who is unrepresented at 10265 Stewartsville Rd, Vinton, VA 24179.
